Privacy

Delete your Laxmee data

This page is for the Laxmee app for Android, published by Laxmee on Google Play. Laxmee has no account: your money data lives only on your phone, and what does leave it carries no name, phone number or email.

How to request deletion

  1. Stop sending anything new. In Laxmee, open Profile → Helping Laxmee and turn off "Share anonymous usage stats" and "Help Laxmee read more banks".
  2. Ask us to delete what was already sent. Email contact@laxmee.in with the subject "Delete my Laxmee data". Because the data carries no name, it is deleted by your installation's random ID; we'll reply with how to find it on your phone.
  3. We confirm. We reply within 7 days and ask Google Firebase to delete the usage stats and crash reports linked to that ID.
  4. Delete what is on your phone. Uninstall Laxmee. This deletes your transactions, accounts, budgets, goals and stored messages. There is no other copy, so this cannot be undone. (Backups you exported yourself are your own files; delete them separately.)

What is deleted

  • On your phone, when you uninstall: everything Laxmee stored, including transactions, balances, payees, budgets, goals, settings and bank SMS it kept.
  • On request: anonymous usage stats (screens opened, a few events, banded counts such as how many accounts you have, approximate city from your IP), crash reports and device details sent with them, and the random installation IDs they are linked to.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Message formats you chose to share are kept to support those bank formats. They have every number, name, UPI ID, email and link removed and are linked only to a random ID that is not shown in the app, so they can't be matched to you.
  • Usage stats and crash reports you don't ask us to delete are removed automatically: Google Analytics after 2 months and Crashlytics crash reports after 90 days.
  • Purchases of Laxmee Plus are held by Google Play under your Google account and its own policy; Laxmee does not store them.
