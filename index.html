Laxmee.

Reads your bank SMS on your phone and shows where your money goes: spending, income, budgets and goals. No account, no server. Nothing leaves your phone.

Download for Android

Download Laxmee

Android 8 or newer, 64-bit phones.

    Installing

    1. Open the downloaded file. Android asks to let your browser install apps: allow it once.
    2. If Play Protect says the app is unknown, tap More details › Install anyway.
    3. Updating later? Make a backup first (Profile › Backup). Updates keep your data.

    Privacy

    Your SMS, transactions and backups stay on your phone, encrypted. Once a day the app asks this website whether a newer version exists; nothing about you or your money is sent, though like any website, laxmee.in sees your phone's internet address. You can turn this off in Profile › Updates.

    Supported banks so far: HDFC, SBI, Bank of India, IDFC FIRST, Axis credit cards. Messages from other banks wait in Needs review.
