Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated 11 October 2026. This policy covers the Laxmee app for Android and this website.

Draft for review before Laxmee's public release. It describes how the app works today.

The short version

  • Laxmee reads your bank's transaction SMS on your phone and stores what it finds on your phone, encrypted.
  • There is no Laxmee account, no Laxmee server and no analytics. We never receive your messages, transactions or balances.
  • Uninstalling Laxmee deletes everything it stored.

What the app reads

With your permission, Laxmee reads SMS from business senders (sender IDs like JM-HDFCBK) that mention money, and listens for new ones so it stays up to date. Messages from people are never opened. Of the business messages it reads:

  • Bank transaction alerts are kept so your history can be re-read when the app improves, and so you can see the original message behind each transaction.
  • One-time passwords and offers are recognised and their text is discarded straight away.
  • Messages from non-bank businesses are not kept, unless they read like a payment, in which case they are kept so they can be read correctly if that sender turns out to be a bank.

Where it is stored

Everything Laxmee stores is in an encrypted database on your phone. The encryption key is created on your phone and kept in Android's secure key storage; it never leaves the device. Laxmee turns off Android's automatic cloud backup for its data, so it is not copied to Google Drive.

What leaves your phone

  • Update check. Once a day, the app asks https://laxmee.in/latest.json whether a newer version exists. The request contains nothing about you; like any web request, it reveals your phone's internet address to the site's host (GitHub Pages). You can turn this off in Profile › Updates. Copies installed from Google Play are updated by Google Play.
  • Backups and exports you make. An encrypted backup (protected by a password only you know) or an Excel/CSV export goes only where you choose to save or share it. Exports are not encrypted, and the app says so.
  • Optional features. If a future version offers on-device "Smarter reading", it will be off by default; turning it on downloads a model once, and your messages are still read on your phone only. Purchases, when offered, are handled by Google Play.

Laxmee has no crash reporting, analytics or advertising.

Permissions

  • Read and receive SMS: to build your transactions from bank alerts.
  • Fingerprint or face: only if you turn on app lock.
  • Internet: for the update check described above.

This website

laxmee.in is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics or third-party scripts, and its fonts are served from laxmee.in itself. GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy statement.

Your control

  • Delete a transaction, change any category, or hide an account that isn't yours.
  • Turn off SMS access in Android settings at any time; Laxmee keeps working with what it has and with entries you add by hand.
  • Uninstall to delete everything Laxmee stored.

Children

Laxmee is meant for adults managing their own money and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
