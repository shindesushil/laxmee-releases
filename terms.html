Using Laxmee

Laxmee helps you see and organise your own money from the transaction messages your bank sends. You may use it for your personal, non-commercial purposes.

Not financial advice

Laxmee shows what your messages say and the totals that follow from them. It does not give financial, investment, tax or legal advice. Decisions you make based on what you see in Laxmee are your own.

Accuracy

Laxmee reads bank messages automatically and can get things wrong: a format it doesn't know, a missing message, or an estimate for a foreign-currency charge. Check important figures against your bank's statement. Anything Laxmee isn't sure about is shown in Needs review.
