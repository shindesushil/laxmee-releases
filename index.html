How it works

From your bank's SMS to a clear picture, in three steps.

  1. Bring your picture together

    Allow SMS access once. Laxmee reads the transaction alerts your bank already sends and builds your accounts and history, right on your phone.

  2. See your patterns

    Spending by category and by day, income, recurring payments and how this month compares with the last.

  3. Move toward your goals

    Set budgets and savings goals, keep an eye on what's coming up, and watch your progress fill in.

Questions

Good to know.

What is Laxmee?

An Android app that shows where your money goes. It reads the transaction SMS your bank sends, on your phone, and turns them into spending, income, budgets, recurring payments and savings goals.

What can I track with Laxmee?

Spending and income by category, balances for each account, subscriptions, EMIs, SIPs and bills, a monthly statement with opening and closing balances, foreign card charges, and anything you add by hand, like cash.

Can I manage budgets and savings goals?

Yes. Set monthly limits per category and see what's left each day. Create goals with a target and an optional date, link an investment like a SIP, and add money by hand.

Is Laxmee available on mobile?

Laxmee is an Android app (Android 8 or newer). It is in a closed test with a small group right now, and a public release on Google Play comes next. There is no iPhone version yet.

Does Laxmee connect directly to my bank?

No. Laxmee never asks for a banking login and doesn't connect to any bank. It reads the SMS alerts your bank already sends. Today it understands HDFC, SBI, Bank of India, IDFC FIRST and Axis credit card messages; messages it can't read yet wait in Needs review for you to add or dismiss.

How is my financial information handled?

It stays on your phone, in an encrypted database whose key never leaves the device. Laxmee has no accounts, no server and no analytics. The only routine network request is a daily check of laxmee.in for a newer version, which you can turn off; nothing about you is sent. Read the privacy policy.
